# Internet outage troubleshooting

If the connection drops, first check for a known outage in your area on the status page. Then power-cycle the modem and router: unplug both, wait 30 seconds, plug in the modem first and wait for its lights to settle, then the router.

Check cable connections and try a wired device to rule out Wi-Fi. If the modem's online light stays off after a restart, or drops repeat daily, open a ticket so a technician can check line quality.
