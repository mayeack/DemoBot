# When to seek urgent or emergency care

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room for chest pain or pressure, trouble breathing, sudden weakness or numbness on one side, slurred speech, severe headache with stiff neck, confusion, fainting, uncontrolled bleeding, or thoughts of harming yourself.

Same-day urgent care is appropriate for a fever that does not respond to over-the-counter medicine, a possible fracture or sprain, a cut that may need stitches, or worsening symptoms after several days of home care. A primary-care visit fits ongoing or non-urgent concerns and medication questions.
