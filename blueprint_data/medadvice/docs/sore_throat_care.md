# Sore throat and cold self-care

Most sore throats and colds are viral and improve within 7 to 10 days. Rest, fluids, warm salt-water gargles, throat lozenges, honey in warm tea (adults), and a humidifier ease symptoms. Over-the-counter acetaminophen or ibuprofen can reduce throat pain and fever.

See a clinician if symptoms last beyond 10 days, if there is a high or persistent fever, white patches on the tonsils, difficulty breathing or swallowing, drooling, or a rash. A rapid strep test decides whether antibiotics are needed; antibiotics do not help viral infections.
