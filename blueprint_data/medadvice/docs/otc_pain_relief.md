# Over-the-counter pain and fever relief

Acetaminophen and ibuprofen are the two common over-the-counter options for mild pain and fever in adults. Follow the label directions for dose and spacing; do not combine products that contain the same ingredient. Ibuprofen is best taken with food and avoided by people with stomach ulcers, kidney problems, or on blood thinners unless a clinician approves.

Seek care rather than self-treating if pain is severe, worsening, or accompanied by chest pain, shortness of breath, confusion, a stiff neck, or a fever above 103 F (39.4 C). Children's dosing depends on weight; this service does not provide pediatric dosing.
