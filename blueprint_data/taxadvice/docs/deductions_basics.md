# Standard versus itemized deductions

Most filers take the standard deduction. Itemizing only helps when qualifying expenses (mortgage interest, state and local taxes up to the cap, charitable gifts, large medical expenses above the income threshold) add up to more than the standard amount for the filing status.

Keep receipts and statements for anything itemized. Above-the-line adjustments such as retirement contributions or student-loan interest apply whether or not you itemize. This is general information, not a filing position.
